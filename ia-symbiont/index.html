IA Symbiont

Your IA is your companion — your voice and your persona. In the Golden Era, everyone had one, like a cell phone. Pick the one that feels like yours. It will roam the pages with you: tap it to pet it, fling it to throw it.

Choose your IA

More voices and personas will be added later. A voice preview is coming — for now the pick plays a chime.

Your Symbiont follows you everywhere...

Once you pick an IA, it shows up on every page of the terminal (except the game) and roams with you. You can also pick it from the Avatar Editor, where it sits as a guide while you build your avatar.
