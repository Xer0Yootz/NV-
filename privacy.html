Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

What we collect

We collect only what the site needs to run:

  • Account info from your sign-in provider. When you sign in with GitHub or Google, the provider shares the basic account details needed to sign you in, such as your email address and display name. Sign-in is handled by Supabase.
  • The username you choose. On the hub you can choose a display name. That name is tied to your account on this site.
  • Chat messages. Messages you send in the hub chat are stored so the conversation can be shown and so Neon can reply.
  • Location, only if you share it. The hub ticker can show the time, date, weather, and tides for where you are. It uses your browser location only if you allow it. If you do not, it falls back to an approximate location from your connection, and then to Oakland as a default.

Cookies

This site uses cookies only to keep you signed in. That is the whole of it. We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking.

How we use your information

We use what we collect to run the site: to keep you signed in, to show you the hub and the ticker, and to make the chat work. We do not use your information for anything else.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and chat data only as long as the site needs it to work. If you want to ask about your account or your data, including asking for it to be removed, contact bookings@jasmineinfiniti.com and we will help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we do, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your data: bookings@jasmineinfiniti.com.

See also our Terms of Service.
