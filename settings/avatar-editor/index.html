Camera presets reframe without touching your morphs. Lighting presets restyle the 3-point studio rig (key / fill / rim) and the stage backdrop only — the model and its materials are unchanged. Editing a part moves the camera to it automatically.

Console — history & symmetry Symmetrical

Paired features — eyes, nose, ears — get an L and an R slider. While Symmetrical is on they mirror each other; switch to Asymmetrical to push one side past the other (the morph itself is shared, so the split shows as a fine lateral offset on that feature). Both sides are stored in the Face ID.
